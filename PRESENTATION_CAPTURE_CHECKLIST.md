# Seven-Slide Presentation Capture Checklist

Use actual Cronoscope screens only. Confirm the selected event’s live Project
tab accepts a presentation of at most seven slides before final export.

1. **Cronoscope introduction** — home view with project identity and the clear
   evidence-first purpose.
2. **Genuine SPHEREx image viewer** — local viewer with a labelled retained
   SPHEREx product, release, detector, and observation context visible.
3. **Wavelength and coordinate inspection** — science panel with a selected
   pixel’s RA/Dec, intensity, variance, flags, and local WCS-WAVE context;
   label WCS-WAVE as context, not a source spectrum.
4. **Two-epoch observations** — a pair view with both observation times and
   the release/provenance context.
5. **Blink and difference** — registered B minus A display or blink controls;
   retain the on-screen non-detection/research-context warning.
6. **Moving-object research limits** — frozen Horizons predicted position and
   the explicit statement that it is prediction/research context, not a
   confirmed detection or discovery.
7. **Future vision** — one credited Hubble gallery image. Label it illustrative
   and non-SPHEREx, with the required NASA/ESA credit.

Before finalizing: remove private paths, avoid unlabelled screenshots, preserve
source credits, verify readable text at presentation scale, and do not show or
claim the DR1 102-channel cube as integrated.
